## Design Context

### Users

Developers and technically curious readers following Andrew's builds. Some
will run Dolittle on NVIDIA or Apple Silicon machines; the project site also
explains the work to readers without that hardware. It supports discussion
and exploration alongside the running experiment.

### Brand Personality

Curious, practical, precise. Explain why the build matters and what the
observations support. Use Andrew's voice for public prose, grounded in the
supplied brief and actual project records.

### Aesthetic Direction

Use the Plainsight family shell shared with Cradle and Serenity: navy header/footer,
ivory reading surface, brass accents, and self-hosted Plex Serif with restrained Plex Mono
navigation. Dolittle's existing engraved parrot artwork
provides its identity. Favor reading, explanation, and open editorial layouts.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the question and an accessible explanation; writing is the spine.
2. Explanations and explorations can stand on their own. Milestones record
   working capabilities without dictating the publication sequence.
3. Distinguish captured thinking, NLA interpretations, and editorial commentary.
4. Preserve readable pages, keyboard access, mobile navigation, and reduced motion.
5. Link to the Plainsight hub for the broader project family. Keep setup and
   platform information aligned with the implemented CUDA and Metal application.
