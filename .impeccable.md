## Design Context

### Users

Developers and technically curious readers following Andrew's builds. Some
will run Dolittle on their own NVIDIA machines; the project site must also
explain the work to readers without that hardware. The user wants room for
discussion and exploration before and between working milestones.

### Brand Personality

Curious, practical, precise. Explain why the build matters and what the
observations support. Use Andrew's voice for public prose, grounded in the
supplied brief and actual project records.

### Aesthetic Direction

Andrew requested a site closer to Cradle, borrowing Serenity's milestones.
Retain their Plainsight family shell: navy header/footer, ivory reading
surface, brass accents, and self-hosted Plex Serif with restrained Plex Mono
navigation. This is intentional continuity with the user-selected references,
not a new font-selection exercise. Dolittle's existing engraved parrot artwork
provides its identity. Favor reading, explanation, and open editorial layouts.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the question and an accessible explanation; writing is the spine.
2. Explanations and explorations can stand on their own. Milestones record
   working capabilities without dictating the publication sequence.
3. Distinguish captured thinking, NLA interpretations, and editorial commentary.
4. Preserve readable pages, keyboard access, mobile navigation, and reduced motion.
5. Link to the Plainsight hub for the broader project family. Show honest build
   status, and do not imply the GPU application is already available.
